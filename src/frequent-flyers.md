# Frequent Flyers

The 'Frequent Flyers' group is made up from the top points scorers after the preceding December or June. The
group may co-opt other club members as appropriate. This group is responsible for taking policy decisions on
behalf of the club.

## How points are awarded

| Activity | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Leading a ride for the first time | 20 |
| Leading a ride | 15 |
| Acting as backstop for a significant part of the ride | 8 |
| Participating in a club ride | 5 |
| Participating in part of a ride | 1–2 |
| Attending a planning meeting | 4 |
| Attending a social meeting | 4 |
| Bike aid/first aid on a ride | 2 |

Points are awarded for prearranged club events, which generally means those announced in the
[diary]({{ '/diary/' | url }}). Ad hoc rides organised by club members, even if arranged through the club
WhatsApp group, do not qualify.
