After a group photo seventeen of us set off from the Little Green Dragon and met up with our eighteenth
team member at Arnos Park. With the weather forecast good we were in good spirits as we took an urban route
to Richmond Park via Finchley, Hampstead Garden Suburb, Golders Green, Cricklewood, Acton and Chiswick. On
arriving at the Park we cycled halfway round to Pembroke Lodge where we stopped for lunch. In spite of a
large queue at the café it didn't take us too long to get served and we enjoyed our food in the sun at the
tables outside. This wasn't a difficult ride but the toughest of the hills proved to be in the park itself.
We encountered another challenging hill on our way back to the Roehampton Gate exit, after which it was
mostly plain sailing back to the start. We crossed the Thames at Putney Bridge before heading towards South
Kensington and through Hyde Park to Marble Arch. As we approached Regents Park we were forced to walk our
bikes for a short while and take a slight detour to reach the York Gate entrance. This was due to road
closures for a temporary street fair which hadn't been present on the recces. We made a comfort stop at the
entrance to the Broad Walk path in Regents Park where, in spite of a daunting queue, one of our team was
unable to resist the temptation of purchasing and consuming an ice cream. This slightly delayed our return
but we made good time for the rest of the ride through Camden, Hornsey, Wood Green and back to the Little
Green Dragon. Here we were given a warm welcome and treated to plenty of delicious food and drink.

This was the first of two rides held in memory of our late friend Les Bailey, raising money for the North
London Hospice — see the [second ride, to High Beech]({{ '/ride-reports/2026-06-14-les-bailey-memorial-ride-2-high-beech/' | url }}),
held the same day.
