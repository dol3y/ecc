We met at Trent Park all psyched up for a long ride and hoping we would miss the forecast afternoon
showers. Riding out on Cockfosters and Waggon Roads, we faced our first decision when Dancers Hill Road was
closed. But it looked passable by bike and indeed this was the case, as the holes only covered half of the
road. Not sure what was going on, as it is only about a year ago this road was closed for months. We
continued through Potters Bar and onto the A1000 to Hatfield. Not a lot of fun riding on a busy main road
but it meant we reached Hatfield and puncture number 1 in good time. This fixed, we continued to Welwyn
Garden City for a short break, and from here the ride became more interesting. The loop north of WGC took
us past the [Arts and Crafts church](https://www.ayotstpeter.com/st-peters-church/) at Ayot St Peter and
then into Codicote itself. This stretch was enlivened by a deer crossing the road and a red kite taking off
just in front of us. A short (and steep) way out of Codicote took us to Spokes cyclists' café where we
stopped for various toasted delights including some very overcooked poached eggs. We returned through
Woolmer Green, Tewin, Welwyn Garden City (puncture No 2), Hatfield then back onto the A1000 before diverting
off to the start of Coopers Lane Road where most riders left for the Enfield area, whilst two others rode on
to Barnet. A long ride of about 47 miles but very satisfying, and the showers held off.
