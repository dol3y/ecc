We gathered at the Beggars Hollow café ready for a short, half-day ride but one that promised a variety of
terrain. Setting off initially through the abandoned Whitewebbs golf course, we passed through Crews Hill
before reaching Coopers Lane Road towards Potters Bar. Part way up we diverted onto a woodland path that
runs vaguely parallel to the road, before emerging by the top. A bit of a zig-zag through Potters Bar took
us out along Baker Street, then along Dancers Hill Road to Waggon Road. Whilst descending Waggon Road we
passed another group of cyclists coming from the other direction, who the leader identified as part of the
Hampstead Garden Suburb U3A cycle group. Continuing down an overgrown path to Hadley Wood then Beech Hill
(down) and Ferny Hill (up) before turning onto the new cycle path to the Ridgeway. We finished up passing
Rectory Farm and into Hilly Fields Park, where some of us continued to Beggars Hollow for a coffee before
heading home.
