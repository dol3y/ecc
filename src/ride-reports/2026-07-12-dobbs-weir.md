With the weather still warm a large group of us assembled at Beggars Hollow and set off towards Forty Hall
and the A10 cycle path. We sadly came across a couple of, presumably, homeless people asleep in the
underpass who appeared to have set up home there. They had thoughtfully left room for us to cycle through
on our bikes. After passing Cedars Park we turned down Theobalds Lane and crossed back over the A10 using
the footbridge. It wasn't long before we encountered the first of a very small number of hills, which took
us up Park Lane and Park Lane Paradise. Whilst some of us relished this challenge, there were a few
mutterings heard from others. After this, though, we could enjoy cruising down Holy Cross Hill and
travelling through country lanes up to White Stubbs Lane and down Baas Hill. From here we took the side
streets to Broxbourne High Road, down Mill Lane and through to the Old Nazing Road. As we approached our
destination we had to take a short detour due to overhead cable work but we arrived at Dobbs Weir in good
time. Here we enjoyed an extended lunch break, with some choosing to eat in the café while others plumped
for a liquid lunch in the pub. On the way back we cycled through Lea Valley Park with Clayton Hill being
the only incline of any note. We stopped for ice creams at Fishers Green Lane, and from Waltham Abbey we
headed for Gunpowder Park and Enfield Island Village before cycling through Albany Park and Enfield
Crematorium on our way back to Beggars Hollow. A few of us stayed to listen to the concert (60s music by the
Soul Agents) taking place at Hilly Fields.
