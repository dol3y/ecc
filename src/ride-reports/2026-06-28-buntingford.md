A scorching hot day, with a train from Grange Park to Hertford North. We rode off about 10am clockwise on
country lanes via Bramfield, Datchworth, Walkern, Cromer Windmill (photo stop). Into Buntingford about
11:30 with lunch outside at some tearooms in the main street. Return ride by Hare Street, Hay Street,
Puckeridge, Dane End and into the station about 2:45, just in time to catch a train.
