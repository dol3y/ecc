Our presentation evening on 10th September was a double celebration. First, our Les Bailey fund-raising
ride last June raised over £1000 for the North London Hospice in memory of Les. We presented this sum to a
volunteer from the hospice. Secondly, ECC was formed in September 1996, so this was our 30th birthday. Past
and present members, including some who were founder members back then, joined to share memories.
