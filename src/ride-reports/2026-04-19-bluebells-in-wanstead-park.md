Today's ride was to see the bluebells in Wanstead Park. A large group rode from Edmonton, along cycle paths
and quiet roads through Walthamstow, Leytonstone and Wanstead to Chalet Wood in Wanstead Park, where we saw
loads of bluebells in all their glory. We were fortunate to have lovely sunny weather for our coffee stop at
the tea hut in the park, before returning via Leytonstone, Leyton and Walthamstow back to our starting point
in Edmonton.
