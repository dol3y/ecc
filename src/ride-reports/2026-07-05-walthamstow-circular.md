Yet another hot ride, from Edmonton Leisure Centre east via Walthamstow Wetlands to the Pumphouse, a museum
of old local engineering. Onward to God's Own Junkyard of salacious and quirky old West End neon signs.
Several microbreweries there too. Lunch at Lloyd Park, near the William Morris museum. Back to Edmonton
about 2:30pm.
