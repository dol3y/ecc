Nine of us set off from Whitewebbs Café on a chilly, but dry, morning. Passing through Enfield Crematorium
and along Ordnance Road we made our way to Gunpowder Park. Soon after cycling through Waltham Abbey we
encountered Horseshoe Hill closely followed by Crown Hill, after which we were very nicely warmed up. We
continued to Epping Uplands and along Epping Road to the outskirts of Harlow. On arriving at the Park we
found the café area extremely busy and occupied by a large number of greyhounds. Apparently, we had chosen
the day of the
[Great Global Greyhound Walk](https://greatglobalgreyhoundwalk.co.uk/) for our ride! We joined the long
queue for food and drink and managed to squeeze ourselves in amongst the greyhounds and their owners to
enjoy our lunch outside. I must say the greyhounds were very well behaved, probably better than some of us.
On the way back we passed Roydon and cycled through the Lea Valley Park to Cheshunt. We finished the ride
with a short spell on the New River Path before going our separate ways near to where we started.
