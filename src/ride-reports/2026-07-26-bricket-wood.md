After weeks without rain the first drops fell about 15 minutes before the start. But we thought it would
only be a shower, so we delayed for 10-15 minutes and indeed rain did stop. There were some threatening
looking clouds at times during the ride but rain did indeed hold off for the rest of the day. Bad news for
gardeners! We set off along a familiar route of Waggon Road, Trotters Bottom and Black Lion Hill before
crossing the M25 to Colney Street and Smug Oak Lane. We were a little ahead of schedule, and as the pub at
the planned stop was not yet open we stopped for a photo opportunity at some stepping stones across the
River Ver. On to The Gate pub where we were made very welcome, although the menu and beer options left
something to be desired. After lunch we continued through Bricket Wood and past the
[Picture House](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Picture_House%2C_Bricket_Wood) where we stopped
to admire the terracotta reliefs. Continuing we skirted the edge of Watford, through the grounds of Wall
Hall, across roadworks at Letchmore Heath before finishing through Radlett, Borehamwood and Barnet.

Another warm day riding north west on Peter's route. Arriving after 12:00, lunch outside in the beer garden.
Return leg through Bricket Wood past the sporting-themed house. Back near Oakwood about 2:30pm.
