Our quartet left Edmonton Leisure Centre in dull, slightly drizzly weather, towards Meridian Way path,
southwards via Walthamstow Wetlands as rain eased off! We decided to carry on past the café there and do the
full ride. Riding east round Hackney Marshes, then south in the park to the Greenway Box Café at 11:10. A
light lunch before checking the Orbit slide prices. Ride back to Edmonton by 1:30pm.
