Today's ride was largely off road to the Woodberry Wetlands via a roundabout route and back. A large group
rode from Edmonton, along cycle paths and quiet roads through Ally Pally, along the Muswell Hill section of
the Parkland Walk, up to Highgate Station, along the main section of the Parkland Walk to Finsbury Park,
then round the West Reservoir to the Wetlands. There we met one of the riders who'd missed the start and
made her own way to join us for coffee! Our route back took us through Stamford Hill, over to the River
Lea, then back along the towpath to our starting point in Edmonton. On the way we had a puncture to repair,
which slightly delayed us. But overall a pleasant ride.
