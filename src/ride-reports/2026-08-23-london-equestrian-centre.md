We started off along a familiar route of Cockfosters Road, Waggon Road and Trotters Bottom to the A1
horsebridge, then past the Mops and Brooms pub to Borehamwood. Here we took the backstreets before ending up
by the station, then over the railway bridge and up the long hill to Barnet Lane. A short delay for an
errant chain, then on to Edgwarebury Lane. This drops steeply down to a rural oasis in the centre of Barnet,
but the ostensibly paved surface can only be described as atrocious! Safely negotiated, we rode on the
cycle path alongside Edgware Way to Apex Corner, which fortunately has a cyclists and pedestrians underpass.
Up the steep hill through the grounds of Mill Hill School and along Partingdale Lane took us to the London
Equestrian Centre, where last year we had enjoyed lunch in the stables café. But this time the café was
closed — we had half expected it to be, as it had been closed on the recce ride. A stable hand told us that
this was because the chef was unwell — if so we wish him or her well and hope that the next time we pass
full service will be restored. Instead we continued to Woodside Park where we found a very accommodating
café, although they had sold out of chocolate croissants. After a refreshment break we rode back to Oak
Hill Park then on to our separate homeward destinations.
