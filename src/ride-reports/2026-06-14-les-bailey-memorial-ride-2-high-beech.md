We set off from Firs Farm Community Hub café at 11:00, after some had waved off
[ride one]({{ '/ride-reports/2026-06-14-les-bailey-memorial-ride-1-richmond-park/' | url }}) from the Little
Green Dragon. Riding eastwards to Edmonton, South Chingford, heading to Woodford. After a short ascent to
Epping New Road we rode to the forest and Fairmead Road up to the motorbikers' café for lunch. Then at 2pm
to High Beach church for tea, cakes, and bell ringing. Returning to the Little Green Dragon pub at 3:30 to
await the return of ride 1 from Richmond Park. We then enjoyed food and drinks, some great baking by Pat and
raising a glass to our late friend Les. A record amount was raised for the North London Hospice by these
rides.
