This was a ride the leader had wanted to try since the New River Path opened almost a year ago. Twelve of
us set off from Bullsmore Lane and cycled on a beautiful bright day through Cedars Park and the Lee Valley
Park, followed by Gunpowder Park! Lots of greenery and a stop for some refreshments, and we had a convivial
ride ending up in Krispy Kreme in Enfield for a doughnut and a coffee. Then we cycled through Enfield Park
and the last stretch of the New River Path. Big shout out to Cathy and Geoff, who trialled this tour several
times with Rosie and endured many a visit to Krispy Kreme — thank you so much!
