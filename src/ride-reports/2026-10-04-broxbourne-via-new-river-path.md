Today's ride was another first for the club... a ride along the New River Path all the way to Broxbourne.
We set off from Enfield Town Park up to the start of the Enfield section of the New River Path in
Tenniswood Road and cycled north. Rather unexpectedly the water level was unusually high and there was even
flooding of the path at one point, but we were able to cycle through. On reaching Bullsmore Lane, we went
onto the unexplored section of the path. At first it was quite narrow and it was clear that this section
was incomplete. However as we crossed the M25 the path widened and we continued. The remainder of the path
varied in width and surface, and although it took us through very nice countryside, it was annoying to
discover various kissing gates which slowed down our progress. We reached our stopping point, the Old Mill
Retreat, about 11am and enjoyed a drink and a snack before beginning our return. The route back took us
along the River Lea towpath, around the bird sanctuary and onto Waltham Abbey. From there we cycled through
Gunpowder Park, across to the A10 and back down the New River Path to Enfield.
