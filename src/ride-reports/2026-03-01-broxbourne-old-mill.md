We set off in dull weather from Edmonton Leisure Centre east to Lee Valley from Meridian Way. Towards Lee
Valley Park's farms trail up Clayton Hill with a misty sky and drizzle. Descending to go on road towards the
café by the railway, arriving 11:45. We sat inside for lunch. Returning on paths to Cheshunt, then the A10
cycle path to Enfield as we split up.
