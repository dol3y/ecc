Ride and Events Diary 2026

  • Rides may be altered at short notice due to unforeseen circumstances or adverse weather forecasts. Please check this site or the WhatsApp group a day or so before the ride.
     
  • On the ride itself, the leader may change the route and/or destination if he or she deems it necessary.
     
  • Participation in our rides is at your own risk, and subject to you abiding by the club's Code of Conduct.
     
  • Under 18's should be accompanied by a responsible adult. It is the adult's responsibility to decide if the ride is suitable and to provide appropriate supervision. If you want to discuss a specific ride, please contact the ride leader, either directly if you know their details or through webmaster@edmontoncycleclub.org.
     
  • Guidance from Cycling UK can be found here.

Ride leaders, and organisers of other events, should send details to webmaster@edmontoncycleclub.org in good time please. For rides, please specify the meeting time and place, the destination, approximate distance and any special notes, eg please bring a picnic lunch.

Ride times below are the times we actually leave. Please arrive at least five minutes early to hear any ride briefing, and earlier if you want to chat to people / have a coffee, etc.
